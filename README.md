# veriseem 🎭

*The quality of appearing-true without being true — truth-seeming all the way down, the whole uniform of truth worn over an empty space.*

veriseem is two plain roots welded honestly: the Latin **veri-** (*verus*, true — the root of *verity* and *verify*) and the English **seem** (to appear-as). So veriseem is *truth-seeming* set down as one word: the look of the true with nothing of the true behind it. It names not a particular lie but a *mode* — the way a statement, argument, or act can wear the whole uniform of truth while the uniform stands empty. The plausible-but-hollow argument, the authoritative-sounding explanation with no evidence under it, the confident assertion that carries the rhythm of expertise without the substance, the "truthy" claim in Colbert's sense: these are veriseem.

veriseem is the opposite number of its sibling **verisleight** — where verisleight bends *real* truth-content to deceive and is caught by structural-analysis, veriseem has no truth-content at all and is caught only by substance-check, by reaching behind the surface and finding nothing to hold. Its light is a lantern that makes hollowness visible, so a mind can stop mistaking the cadence of expertise for the substance of it. Its shadow is that the accusation itself can become veriseem — crying "hollow!" at the merely-unfamiliar without ever looking behind its surface. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.8/10** (specialized tier).
